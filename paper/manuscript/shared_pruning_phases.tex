\subsection{Shared Pruning Phases}
\label{sec:shared-phases}

Algorithms~\ref{alg:search-phase}--\ref{alg:recovery-phase} separate
search, checkpoint/mask selection and recovery. Their inputs specify
gate mode, normalization, controller and initialization policies;
the phase logic is shared across architectures. The loss is defined in
Appendix~\ref{app:shared-loss}; architecture-specific gate positions are
given in Appendices~\ref{app:implementation-mobilenetv2} and
\ref{app:implementation-resnet50}.

Let $D$ contain permanently disabled channels and let
$\mathcal H=\{(\theta_t,\phi_t)\}_{t=1}^{T_s}$ be the search history,
including each checkpoint's BatchNorm (BN) state. With group-normalized
opening scores $S$ and optional negative entropy $-\beta\overline H$,
search minimizes $\mathcal L_{\rm CE}+\lambda_t S-\beta\overline H$.
All final runs use sampled straight-through (ST) training masks;
deterministic ST remains an alternative supported mode.
Validation uses deterministic masks. The sparse-prior derivation above
applies under its stated sampling and normalization assumptions.

\begin{algorithm*}[!t]
\caption{Search: learn gate scores and save search checkpoints.}
\label{alg:search-phase}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE Network $f_\theta$; training/validation data; disabled set $D$;
search initialization $\mathcal I_s$; reference accuracies $A_t^{\rm ref}$;
budget $T_s$; gate, objective and controller settings.
\STATE Initialize $\theta$ from $\mathcal I_s$ and fresh gate logits $\phi$;
permanently mask $D$; initialize a fresh optimizer and scheduler.
\STATE $\lambda_1\gets\lambda_0$; $\mathcal H\gets[\,]$; reset controller state.
\FOR{$t=1,\ldots,T_s$}
\STATE Set the epoch loss to $\mathcal L_{\rm CE}+\lambda_t S-\beta\overline H$.
\STATE Train one epoch with sampled/deterministic binary forward masks and ST gradients.
\STATE Measure deterministic validation accuracy $A_t$;
append $(\theta_t,\phi_t)$ to $\mathcal H$.
\IF{$t\leq W$ or $t<w$ or no controller update is scheduled}
\STATE $\lambda_{t+1}\gets\lambda_t$.
\ELSE
\IF{gap mode is paired}
\STATE $\overline\Delta_t\gets w^{-1}\sum_{i=t-w+1}^{t}(A_i^{\rm ref}-A_i)$.
\ELSE
\STATE $\overline\Delta_t\gets A_t^{\rm ref}-w^{-1}\sum_{i=t-w+1}^{t}A_i$.
\ENDIF
\STATE Compute the configured log-step $\eta_t$; update $\lambda_{t+1}$
by Eq.~\eqref{eq:one-shot-controller}, using $\eta_t$ in place of $\eta$.
\ENDIF
\ENDFOR
\RETURN Search history $\mathcal H$ and final reference accuracy $A_{T_s}^{\rm ref}$.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm*}

The fixed-step controller uses its configured $\eta$; the automatic
one-shot setting computes
$\eta=\log(\lambda_{\rm target}/\lambda_0)/\lfloor T_s/3-W\rfloor$,
requiring a positive denominator. Adaptive step boosting follows the
closure-growth rule in Appendix~\ref{app:implementation-mobilenetv2}.
Warmup is included in $T_s$.

\paragraph{Checkpoint and mask selection.}
The selection policy $s$ is either history-feasible or final-score.
For history selection, $\operatorname{MakeMask}_{\geq1}$ keeps scores
above the configured evaluation threshold and enforces the retention
floor, retaining highest-score channels if necessary. The principal
floor keeps at least one channel per group. Each fixed mask is evaluated
with frozen weights and BN; selection performs no training or BN
recalibration. $\operatorname{Params}(m)$ counts the physical gate-free
architecture. Historical selection windows and floors are specified in
Appendix~\ref{app:implementation-resnet50}.

For final-score selection, $\operatorname{Candidates}_{\geq1}$ considers
remaining channels below $\tau$ in threshold mode, or all remaining
channels in budget-only mode. It reserves a highest-score channel if a
group would otherwise become empty. Candidates are processed in increasing
score order. The cap is $\rho P_{\rm probe}$, where $P_{\rm probe}$ counts
the full-width gated model and $c(u)$ is the approximate additive removal
cost of channel $u$. Overlapping channel dependencies make these costs
approximate; the cap is not an achieved compression ratio.

\begin{algorithm*}[!t]
\caption{Checkpoint Selection: choose the search checkpoint and fixed mask.}
\label{alg:selection-phase}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE Search history $\mathcal H$; disabled set $D$; selection policy $s$;
validation data; final reference accuracy $A_{T_s}^{\rm ref}$;
tolerance $\delta_h$; selection windows, retention floor, threshold $\tau$ and optional cap $\rho$.
\IF{$s=\text{history-feasible}$}
\FOR{$t$ in the feasible-candidate or fallback search window}
\STATE $m_t\gets\operatorname{MakeMask}_{\geq1}(\phi_t)$;
$A_t^{\rm mask}\gets\operatorname{Eval}_{\rm val}(\theta_t,m_t)$;
$P_t\gets\operatorname{Params}(m_t)$.
\ENDFOR
\STATE $\mathcal F\gets\{t\text{ in feasible window}:A_t^{\rm mask}\geq A_{T_s}^{\rm ref}-\delta_h\}$.
\IF{$\mathcal F\neq\varnothing$}
\STATE $t^*\in\arg\min_{t\in\mathcal F}P_t$.
\ELSE
\STATE $t^*\in\arg\max_{t\text{ in fallback window}}A_t^{\rm mask}$.
\ENDIF
\STATE $m\gets m_{t^*}$; $R\gets\{u\notin D:m_u=0\}$.
\ELSE
\STATE $t^*\gets T_s$; $Q\gets\operatorname{Candidates}_{\geq1}(\phi_{T_s},D)$;
sort $Q$ by increasing opening score.
\STATE $R\gets\varnothing$;
$B\gets\rho P_{\rm probe}$ if a cap is set, otherwise $B\gets\infty$.
\FOR{channel $u$ in $Q$}
\IF{$c(u)\leq B$}
\STATE $R\gets R\cup\{u\}$; $B\gets B-c(u)$.
\ENDIF
\ENDFOR
\STATE $m_u\gets\mathbf1[u\notin D\cup R]$.
\ENDIF
\RETURN Selected checkpoint $(\theta_{t^*},\phi_{t^*})$, mask $m$, disabled set $D\cup R$ and new removals $R$.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm*}

\paragraph{Recovery.}
$\operatorname{Compact}(\theta_{t^*},m)$ physically removes discarded
channels and gates, copying surviving weights and BN state. Recovery
either inherits that state or replaces it according to $\mathcal I_r$.
Reinitialization may draw new random weights or reload surviving slices
of pretrained weights. BN recalibration is an optional, separately
configured step, performed after the recovery weights are initialized.

\begin{algorithm*}[!t]
\caption{Recovery: train and validate the compact, gate-free network.}
\label{alg:recovery-phase}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE Selected checkpoint $\theta_{t^*}$ and mask $m$; training/validation data;
recovery policy (inherit / restart), initialization $\mathcal I_r$;
BN recalibration budget; recovery budget $T_r$.
\STATE $g\gets\operatorname{Compact}(\theta_{t^*},m)$.
\IF{recovery policy is restart}
\STATE $g\gets\operatorname{Initialize}(g,\mathcal I_r)$.
\ENDIF
\IF{BN recalibration is enabled}
\STATE Recalibrate BN on the configured number of training batches.
\ENDIF
\STATE Initialize a fresh optimizer and scheduler.
\STATE Train $g$ for $T_r$ epochs using cross-entropy without gates.
\RETURN Best validation checkpoint of $g$ during recovery.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm*}
