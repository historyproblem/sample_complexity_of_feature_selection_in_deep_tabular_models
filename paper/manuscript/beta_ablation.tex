\paragraph{Effect of entropy regularization.}
With all other settings held fixed, changing $\beta$ is accompanied by
a redistribution of opening scores
(Figure~\ref{fig:beta-channel-ablation}) and retained channels.
In the examined runs, positive $\beta$ values yield a smaller fraction
of retained output channels than $\beta=0$.
